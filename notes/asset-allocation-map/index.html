资产配置不是一道选择题：从 60/40 到全天候，再到 L1Asset allocation is not one answer: from 60/40 to All Weather and L1

这不是“哪个组合最好”的排行榜。60/40、永久组合、全天候、风险平价、1/N 和趋势覆盖各自解决的问题不同。真正决定结果的，是风险如何分配、规则如何执行、约束和成本如何处理，以及结果能否被归因和复核。This is not a league table of portfolios. The 60/40, Permanent Portfolio, All Weather, risk parity, 1/N and trend overlays solve different problems. What decides the outcome is how risk is allocated, how rules are implemented, how constraints and costs are handled, and whether results can be attributed and audited.

  • 基准不是策略，是尺子。60/40 足够简单，但会在股债同跌时失效；没有统一基准，所谓“更好配置”无法判断。A benchmark is a yardstick, not a strategy. The 60/40 is simple and breaks when stocks and bonds fall together; without it, “better allocation” cannot be measured.
  • 资金权重不等于风险权重。权益常在组合里承担远超其资金占比的风险，而全天候也可能把风险集中到长债。Capital weights are not risk weights. Equities often carry more risk than their capital share, while All Weather can concentrate risk in duration.
  • 越复杂不等于越好。1/N 在多项测试中击败复杂优化器，说明估计误差和约束落地往往比公式更重要。More complex is not better by default. 1/N beats many optimisers, showing that estimation error and implementation often dominate the formula.
  • 专题已经足够，但必须从文章集合升级成决策地图。现有内容已覆盖基准、替代方案、风险、执行和负结果；缺的是一个统一入口和 L1 这种端到端案例。The series is already sufficient, but it needs to become a decision map. It covers benchmarks, alternatives, risk, implementation and negative results; what was missing was one entry point and an end-to-end case such as L1.

02

再分风险Allocate the risk

资金权重只是表面。真正的配置问题是风险预算如何落到资产和因子上。Capital weights are cosmetic. The real allocation question is where the risk budget lands.

The missing piece was not more strategies, but a reading path from benchmark choice to implementation and governance. Adding another strategy now has less value than connecting the existing work, aligning conventions, and proving the process through one continuously updated portfolio case.
