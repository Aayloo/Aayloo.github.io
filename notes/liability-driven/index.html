负债驱动的资产配置：换一个分母，排序就变了Liability-driven allocation: change the denominator and the ranking changes

投资Investing负债驱动Liability-driven资产配置Asset allocation

组合优化默认的目标是「资产涨得多」。但如果投资者背后站着一笔负债——保险的偿付、养老金的给付——目标就变成「比负债涨得多，而且稳」。本文用 1993-02 至 2025-09 共 392 个月的美国数据，把一笔 20 年期定额年金当作负债，让七个组合跑同一段历史。结果不是「负债让收益变低」，而是排序整个换了一遍：资产端夏普第一的 1/N 等权（0.71）在盈余端排第五；资产端夏普垫底的久期匹配（0.26），盈余波动只有 2.15%、funding ratio 最大回撤只有 −2.4%——而纯股是 −58.9%。 Portfolio optimisation assumes the objective is "make the assets grow". If the investor stands behind a liability — an insurer's claims, a pension's benefit payments — the objective becomes "grow faster than the liability, and steadily". This note gives seven portfolios the same 392 months of US data, from February 1993 to September 2025, with a 20-year level annuity as the liability. The result is not that the liability lowers your return; it is that the ranking is rewritten. The best asset-only Sharpe, 1/N at 0.71, ranks fifth on surplus; the worst asset-only Sharpe, duration matching at 0.26, delivers 2.15% surplus volatility and a −2.4% maximum drawdown in the funding ratio — against −58.9% for all-equities.

  • 负债不是常数。10 年期收益率从 6.4% 走到 3.6%，同一笔年金负债的现值涨了 27%，修正久期在 7.9–9.9 年之间漂。任何用「固定目标」做的资产配置，都在跟一个会动的分母比。 The liability is not a constant. As the 10-year yield moved from 6.4% to 3.6%, the present value of the same annuity rose 27% and its modified duration drifted between 7.9 and 9.9 years. Any allocation measured against a fixed target is racing a moving denominator.
  • 久期缺口就是盈余风险，而且是一条 U 形线。资产久期为 0 时，盈余波动 7.54%；逐月把资产久期对准负债久期（约 9 年）降到 2.19%；再往上加久期，波动重新上升。 The duration gap is the surplus risk, and it traces a U. At zero asset duration surplus volatility is 7.54%; matching asset duration to the liability (about nine years) takes it to 2.19%; adding duration beyond that pushes it back up.

At a funding ratio of 100%, even a pure duration-matched book has a 5% historical chance of falling below 100% within a year. At 115% the same 5% tolerance permits 55% equities; at 130%, 80%. The risk budget is not a preference — it is a function of the funding ratio.

摘要本文检验一个问题：当投资者背后有一笔负债时，「资产配置」应当怎么改。样本是 1993-02 至 2025-09 共 392 个月的美国月度数据，与站上其他研究笔记共用同一份公开数据管线（标普 500 全收益、美国国债收益率曲线合成的三条债券腿、LBMA 金价、世界银行商品价格指数）。负债被建模为一笔 20 年期定额年金，按当月 10 年期美国国债收益率平折现，采用恒定到期假设，修正久期样本均值 9.00 年。七个组合从 funding ratio 100% 出发，覆盖纯股、60/40、1/N 等权、风险平价、久期匹配、匹配+成长与盈余最优化。结论分三层：机制上，久期缺口与盈余波动是一条 U 形曲线，最低点在资产久期等于负债久期处；排序上，资产端与盈余端的表现排序几乎不相关，资产端第一的组合在盈余端只排第五；操作上，可承受的风险随 funding ratio 上升而系统性放宽，并且在 funding ratio 100% 时没有任何组合能同时做到「不跌破」与「拿高股票权重」。边界负债是合成的、单一利率折现、不含新业务与退保选择权；资产端只有六条腿、只做多、不加杠杆；全部为美国资产；含交易成本，不含税费与汇率。

AbstractThis note asks a narrow question: if the investor stands behind a liability, what should change in asset allocation? The sample is 392 monthly US observations from February 1993 to September 2025, sharing the same public data pipeline as the other notes on this site (S&P 500 total return, three bond legs synthesised from the Treasury yield curve, the LBMA gold fix, World Bank commodity indices). The liability is modelled as a 20-year level annuity, discounted flat at that month's 10-year Treasury yield under a constant-maturity assumption, with a sample-average modified duration of 9.00 years. Seven portfolios start from a funding ratio of 100%: all-equities, 60/40, 1/N, risk parity, duration matched, duration-matched plus growth, and surplus-optimised. The findings have three layers. Mechanically, the duration gap and surplus volatility trace a U whose minimum sits where asset duration equals liability duration. In ranking, asset-space and surplus-space performance are nearly uncorrelated — the best asset-only portfolio ranks fifth on surplus. Operationally, the tolerable amount of risk rises systematically with the funding ratio, and at a funding ratio of 100% no portfolio can simultaneously avoid a shortfall and carry a large equity weight. Limits: the liability is synthetic, discounted off a single yield, with no new business or policyholder options; the asset menu is six legs, long-only and unlevered; everything is US; costs are included, taxes and FX are not.

01负债是一个会动的分母The liability is a moving denominator

资产配置的教科书流程是：估一份资产的预期收益与协方差，解一个最优化问题，最大化风险调整后的收益。这个流程里没有「谁的钱」这个问题——组合是自成一体的，涨得多就是好。

The textbook allocation process estimates asset returns and a covariance matrix, solves an optimisation, and maximises risk-adjusted return. Nowhere in it does the question "whose money is this" appear — the portfolio stands alone, and more is better.

保险公司与养老金的处境不一样。它们手上的资产对应着一笔确定的、以本币计价的未来支付：保单到期要赔、养老金按月要发。这笔支付流有一个现值，叫做负债；资产除以负债叫做 funding ratio（偿付能力比率）。真正要管的不是资产本身，是这两者之差。

Insurers and pension funds are different. Their assets stand behind a contractual stream of future payments in domestic currency: claims at maturity, monthly benefits. That stream has a present value — the liability — and assets divided by it is the funding ratio. What has to be managed is not the assets but the difference between the two.

这个区别的第一层含义，就足以推翻很多直觉：负债不是常数。它的现值随利率反向变动，变动的幅度由久期决定。本文把负债建模为一笔 20 年期定额年金，按当月的 10 年期美国国债收益率平折现。下图把它拆开：左图是利率与年金现值因子的关系，右两幅是样本内负债现值与修正久期的实际路径。

The first implication of that difference is enough to overturn several intuitions: the liability is not a constant. Its present value moves inversely with rates, and the size of the move is set by duration. Here the liability is a 20-year level annuity discounted flat at that month's 10-year Treasury yield. 所以本文要回答的是三件事：（一）把目标从资产换成盈余，组合的排序会怎么变？（二）如果会变，变的是哪一项指标——收益、波动，还是回撤？（三）给定当前 funding ratio，能拿多少风险是算得出来的吗？

Three questions follow. One: if the objective moves from assets to surplus, how does the ranking change? Two: if it changes, which statistic changes — return, volatility, or drawdown? Three: given today's funding ratio, is the tolerable amount of risk computable?

02数据与方法Data and method

样本与站上其他研究笔记共用同一份公开数据管线：6 条资产腿——股票（标普 500 全收益）、长债（30 年期国债收益率合成，久期 15）、中债（10 年期，久期 7.5）、短债（2 年期，久期 1.9）、黄金（LBMA 伦敦金价）、商品（世界银行能源 / 金属 / 食品价格指数的等权合成）。数据从 1990-02 到 2025-09，前 36 个月用来做第一次估计，样本外从 1993-02 开始，共 392 个月（32.7 年）。原 8 条腿里的三条商品腿在这里合成一条，好让讨论集中在久期上。

The sample shares the public data pipeline used by the other notes: six asset legs — equities (S&P 500 total return), long bonds (synthesised from the 30-year Treasury yield, duration 15), 10-year bonds (duration 7.5), 2-year bonds (duration 1.9), gold (LBMA fix) and commodities (an equal-weighted composite of the World Bank energy, metals and food indices). The data run from February 1990 to September 2025, with the first 36 months used for the initial estimate, so the out-of-sample record starts in February 1993 and runs 392 months, 32.7 years. The three commodity legs of the eight-leg universe are merged into one here, to keep the discussion on duration.

负债的构造是全文最要紧的一段，所以单独写清楚：一笔恒定到期的 20 年期定额年金，按当月的 10 年期国债收益率平折现，修正久期用麦考利久期除以 1+y 得到——这一步必须做，否则和资产端债券腿的口径对不上（站上债券腿的月度收益是「票息 − 久期 × Δy」，那个久期就是修正久期）。

The liability construction is the load-bearing paragraph of this note, so it gets its own: a constant-maturity 20-year level annuity, discounted flat at that month's 10-year Treasury yield, with modified duration computed as Macaulay duration divided by one plus the yield. That last step is not optional — without it the liability is not on the same footing as the bond legs, whose monthly return is "carry − duration × Δy" with that duration being a modified one.

需要说明三个口径。第一，久期匹配不是固定权重：它每个月重算一次，只用长债与短债两腿，把资产久期对准当月的负债久期，所以它的长债权重会在 50%–60% 之间移动。第二，盈余最优化用的是「资产收益减负债收益」后的切点组合，每年 1 月用最近 36 个月重解一次，只做多、权重和为 1。第三，所有组合都从 funding ratio 100% 出发，并像站上其他笔记一样扣掉 1–5 个基点的单边成本。

Three conventions need stating. First, duration matching is not a fixed weight: it is recomputed monthly using only long and short bonds, targeting that month's liability duration, so its long-bond weight moves between roughly 50% and 60%. Second, surplus optimisation uses the tangency portfolio on "asset return minus liability return", re-solved each January on the trailing 36 months, long-only with weights summing to one. Third, every portfolio starts from a funding ratio of 100%, and all of them pay one to five basis points per leg, one way, as elsewhere on this site.

03机制：久期缺口就是盈余风险The mechanism: the duration gap is the surplus risk

把资产和负债放到同一把尺子上，只需要一个定义：盈余收益 s 满足 (1+s) = (1+r资产) ÷ (1+r负债)。当 r 都是小量时，它近似等于两者之差。

Putting assets and liabilities on one ruler takes a single definition: the surplus return s satisfies (1+s) = (1+rasset) ÷ (1+rliability), which for small r is approximately the difference between the two.

现在假设利率瞬时变动 Δy。资产端动 −DA·Δy，负债端动 −DL·Δy，于是盈余动 −(DA − DL)·Δy。也就是说：盈余的风险不来自资产波动，来自两边久期之差。久期缺口是 9 年，利率动 1 个百分点，funding ratio 就动 9%。

Now let rates move by Δy. The asset side moves −DA·Δy, the liability −DL·Δy, so the surplus moves −(DA − DL)·Δy. In other words: surplus risk does not come from asset volatility; it comes from the difference between the two durations. A nine-year gap means a one-point rate move moves the funding ratio nine per cent.

把这条关系画出来，是一条 U 形线。下面这个实验只做一件事：只用长债与短债两腿，把资产久期从 0 年一点一点调到 15 年，其他什么都不变。

Drawn out, that relationship is a U. The experiment below does one thing only: using just long and short bonds, walk asset duration from zero up to fifteen years, changing nothing else.

Beyond that it rises again (5.84% at fifteen), because the assets have become more rate-sensitive than the liability. The minimum of that curve is not a coincidence; it is the definition of immunisation. Read together: matching duration trades about two points of surplus return for five and a half points of surplus volatility.

一个必须写下来的细节：这七个组合里，只有久期匹配和盈余最优化是「按负债设计」的——前者的缺口是 0，后者的缺口是 −1.65 年，因为盈余最优化选出来的权重平均压在 10 年期国债上（89.6%），而 10 年期国债的久期（7.5）略低于负债（9.0）。其余五个组合的久期都是各自资产端逻辑的副产品，没有人替负债考虑过。

One detail has to be written down: only two of the seven are designed around the liability. Duration matching sits at a zero gap; surplus optimisation sits at −1.65 years because the optimiser put an average of 89.6% into 10-year bonds, whose duration of 7.5 falls a little short of the liability's 9.0. The other five portfolios have whatever duration their asset-side logic happened to produce, with nobody answering to the liability.

0432.7 年：七条 funding ratio 路径Thirty-two years: seven funding-ratio paths

现在把七个组合放进同一段历史，起点都是 funding ratio 100%。看两条线就够：一条是终点，一条是路径最差的时候跌到哪里。

Now run all seven through the same history, each starting from a funding ratio of 100%. Two things matter: where each ends up, and how far it fell on the way.

把这张图拆成一句话：资产空间衡量的「效率」，是每单位资产波动换来多少资产收益；盈余空间衡量的，是每单位盈余波动换来多少盈余收益。这两件事在数学上不等价，因为负债本身也在动。而负债的波动是负相关的——利率下行使负债上升，同时使长债上涨——所以一个在资产端看起来很「保守」的组合（久期匹配，资产端夏普 0.26），在盈余端可以很有效率。

Compressed into one sentence: asset space measures how much asset return each unit of asset volatility buys; surplus space measures how much surplus return each unit of surplus volatility buys. These are not equivalent, because the liability moves too. And the liability is negatively correlated with the thing that hedges it — falling rates raise the liability and lift long bonds at the same time — so a portfolio that looks conservative on the asset side (duration matching, asset Sharpe 0.26) can be highly efficient on the surplus side.

为什么会这样？因为久期匹配手里只有长债与短债，而长债在 2021-2023 那段加息里跌了将近一半——资产端它当然是差生。但同一段时间负债也跌了，而且久期缺口是零，两边等量下跌，funding ratio 几乎不动。这就是「资产端跌得难看、盈余端却没事」的完整解释：不是资产没有风险，是这份风险与负债的风险方向一致、数量相同。

Why does this happen? Because duration matching holds only long and short bonds, and long bonds lost nearly half their value through the 2021–2023 hiking cycle — on the asset side it is plainly the worst pupil. Over the same window, though, the liability fell too, and with a zero duration gap the two declines cancel almost exactly, leaving the funding ratio intact. That is the full explanation of "ugly on assets, untouched on surplus": the risk is not absent, it is the same risk, in the same direction, in the same size, as the liability's.

11结论：换分母，换排序，换风险预算Conclusion: a new denominator, a new ranking, a new risk budget

回到开头的三个问题。

Back to the three opening questions.

第一，排序确实会变，而且变得很彻底。资产端夏普排名前四的组合里，有三个在盈余端掉出前四；资产端垫底的久期匹配（0.26）在盈余端排第二低波动。用盈余夏普排，1/N 等权从第一掉到第六。这不是因为久期匹配变好了，而是因为衡量它的那把尺子换了——而换尺子恰恰是负债驱动投资的第一步。

First, the ranking does change, and thoroughly. Three of the top four asset-only portfolios drop out of the surplus top four, while duration matching — dead last on assets at 0.26 — sits second lowest on surplus volatility. Ranked by surplus Sharpe, 1/N falls from first to sixth. Not because duration matching improved, but because the ruler changed — and changing the ruler is precisely the first move in liability-driven investment.

第二，变的主要是回撤，其次才是波动。七个组合的资产端最大回撤跨 −8.8% 到 −50.9%（5.8 倍），盈余端跨 −2.4% 到 −58.9%（24 倍）。久期匹配的资产端回撤是 −30.1%，盈余端只有 −2.4%。同一份风险，在两张报表上的读数可以差一个数量级——这是本文最实用的一句话。

Second, what changes most is the drawdown, then the volatility. Asset drawdowns span −8.8% to −50.9% (a factor of 5.8); surplus drawdowns span −2.4% to −58.9% (a factor of 24). Duration matching drew down 30.1% in assets and 2.4% in surplus. The same risk can read an order of magnitude differently on the two statements — the most practical sentence in this note.

第三，风险预算算得出来，而且答案比直觉保守。funding ratio 100% 时，即使不持有任何股票，一年内跌破 100% 的历史概率仍有 5%；115% 时才允许 55% 的股票，130% 允许 80%。久期缺口是可以写进限额的数字，股票权重应该是 funding ratio 的函数，而不是投资委员会的偏好。

Third, the risk budget is computable, and the answer is more conservative than intuition. At a funding ratio of 100%, even with no equities at all the historical probability of falling short within a year is 5%; only at 115% does 55% equities fit inside that tolerance, and 80% needs 130%. A duration gap is a number you can write into a limit, and the equity weight should be a function of the funding ratio rather than a committee preference.

边界必须写清楚。第一，负债是合成的、单一利率平折现，真实的保险负债有分期限的贴现曲线、退保与续保选择权、以及新业务带来的久期漂移——本文的恒定到期假设是一个简化，它让负债变成一个干净的久期工具，也让结论更容易读，但不能替代真实的精算模型。第二，资产端只有六条腿、只做多、不加杠杆，没有利率互换、没有通胀挂钩债券——真实的负债驱动组合主要靠这两样工具做精细对冲。第三，全部为美国资产与美元负债，没有汇率维度。第四，全部为历史回测，含 1–5 个基点的单边交易成本，不含税费、融资成本与保证金。第五，「跌破 100% 的概率」是用 392 个月的经验频率估计的，样本内只包含了两个完整的加息周期，估计的尾部比真实情况乐观。

The boundaries need stating. One, the liability is synthetic and discounted off a single yield; a real insurance liability has a term structure of discount rates, lapse and renewal options, and duration drift from new business. The constant-maturity assumption is a simplification that turns the liability into a clean duration instrument and makes the results readable, but it is not a substitute for an actuarial model. Two, the asset menu is six legs, long-only and unlevered, with no interest-rate swaps and no inflation-linked bonds — the two tools a real liability-driven portfolio leans on most for fine hedging. Three, everything is US assets against a dollar liability, with no FX dimension. Four, these are historical backtests with one to five basis points per leg, excluding taxes, funding costs and margin. Five, the "probability of falling below 100%" is estimated from 392 monthly observations containing only two complete hiking cycles, so its tail is optimistic relative to reality.

English version

Question. Portfolio optimisation assumes the objective is asset growth. An insurer or a pension fund stands behind a contractual stream of payments in domestic currency; its present value is the liability, and assets divided by it is the funding ratio. What has to be managed is the difference. This note models the liability as a constant-maturity 20-year level annuity discounted flat at that month's 10-year US Treasury yield, with modified duration equal to Macaulay duration divided by one plus the yield. Over the sample the modified duration averages 9.00 years and drifts between 7.9 and 9.9, while the 10-year yield falls from 6.4% to 3.6% and lifts the liability's present value by 27%.

Data and seven portfolios. Six asset legs — equities, 30-year, 10-year and 2-year Treasuries, gold and a commodity composite — over 428 months from February 1990, with the first 36 used for the initial estimate, leaving 392 out-of-sample months (32.7 years) from February 1993. The seven portfolios are all-equities, 60/40, 1/N, risk parity, duration matched, duration-match-plus-growth, and surplus-optimised; each starts from a funding ratio of 100% and pays one to five basis points per leg.

Result 1 — the duration gap is the surplus risk. Surplus return satisfies (1+s) = (1+rasset) ÷ (1+rliability), so to first order the surplus moves by −(DA − DL)·Δy. Sweeping asset duration from zero to fifteen years with nothing else changed traces a U: surplus volatility reads 7.54% at zero duration, bottoms at 2.19% at nine years — the liability's modified duration — and rises to 5.84% at fifteen. Surplus return climbs monotonically across the same sweep, from 2.12% to 4.12%: matching duration trades about two points of return for five and a half points of volatility.

Result 2 — the ranking is rewritten. All-equities carries a −9.01 year gap, so a two-point rate fall costs its funding ratio 18.0%; duration matching carries a 0.00 gap and reads zero across the row. Over the full sample 1/N leads on asset Sharpe at 0.71 and duration matching trails at 0.26 — and in surplus space duration matching's volatility is 2.15% (second lowest) against 1/N's 6.96% (fifth). Ranked by surplus Sharpe: duration-match-plus-growth 0.54, surplus-optimised 0.52, 60/40 0.51, all-equities 0.49, duration matching 0.47, 1/N 0.45, risk parity 0.27. The best asset-only portfolio ranks sixth on surplus.

Result 3 — the drawdown spread is twenty-four-fold. Asset drawdowns span −8.8% to −50.9%, a factor of 5.8. Funding-ratio drawdowns span −2.4% for duration matching to −58.9% for all-equities, a factor of 24 — and all-equities' surplus drawdown is worse than its asset drawdown, because it carried an unhedged duration gap into the fall. Duration matching lost 30.1% on assets while its funding ratio lost 2.4%: same risk, same direction, same size as the liability's.

Result 4 — the risk budget is computable, and more conservative than intuition. At a funding ratio of 100%, even a pure duration-matched book has a 5% historical probability of falling below 100% within a year, so a 5% shortfall tolerance permits zero equities. At 115% the same tolerance permits 55%; at 130%, 80%; at 140%, 100%. Under a 1% tolerance, nothing above a 10% equity weight is available until the funding ratio passes 103%, and 65% is the ceiling at 140%.

Result 5 — the environment decides. In the second easing leg (2001–2012, yields down 3.90 points) the liability was the problem: all-equities' funding ratio fell 5.1% while duration matching gained 42.2% and 1/N 78.1%. In the fast hiking of 2022 onward (+2.62 points), all seven improved — even all-equities at +33.9% — because the liability fell further than equities did. Through the zero-rate era (2013–2021) duration matching gained only 17.5% against 302.0% for equities, showing how expensive the foregone term premium becomes when rates are pinned. Across 333 rolling five-year windows, duration matching and surplus optimisation were positive in 100% of them, with the narrowest distributions; all-equities had the highest median (+11.30%) but was negative in 27.3%.

Implication. A duration gap is a number that can be written directly into a risk limit, and the equity weight should be a function of the funding ratio rather than a committee preference. Limitations. The liability is synthetic and discounted off a single yield, without lapse or renewal options or new-business duration drift; the asset menu is six legs, long-only and unlevered, with no swaps or inflation-linked bonds; everything is US; costs are included, taxes, funding and margin are not; and the shortfall probabilities come from 392 months containing only two complete hiking cycles, so the tail is optimistic.

附录 A · 数据、口径与已知偏差Appendix A · data, conventions and known biases

一：负债的构造。一笔 20 年期定额年金（每年支付 1 个单位），按当月 10 年期美国国债收益率平折现：Lt = Σk=1..20 (1 + yt)−k。采用恒定到期假设——每年新业务补上滚出的部分——所以久期不随时间缩短，样本内修正久期落在 7.9–9.9 年。注意这里用的是修正久期（麦考利 ÷ (1+y)），因为站上资产端债券腿的月度收益是「票息 − 久期 × Δy」，那个久期就是修正久期；两边口径不一致会直接让久期匹配解错。

1: constructing the liability. A 20-year level annuity (one unit a year) discounted flat at that month's 10-year Treasury yield: Lt = Σk=1..20 (1 + yt)−k. A constant-maturity assumption — new business replaces what rolls off — keeps duration from shortening, leaving it between 7.9 and 9.9 years. Note the use of modified duration (Macaulay ÷ (1+y)): the site's bond legs earn "carry − duration × Δy" with modified duration, and mixing the two conventions breaks the duration match outright.

二：资产端。股票＝标普 500 全收益（Yahoo Finance）；三条债券腿由美国财政部收益率曲线合成，久期分别取 15 / 7.5 / 1.9；黄金＝LBMA 伦敦金价；商品＝世界银行 Pink Sheet 能源、金属、食品三条指数的月度收益等权合成。现金＝3 个月国库券。

2: the asset side. Equities are the S&P 500 total return index (Yahoo Finance); the three bond legs are synthesised from the US Treasury yield curve at durations of 15, 7.5 and 1.9; gold is the LBMA London fix; commodities are the equal-weighted monthly return of the World Bank Pink Sheet energy, metals and food indices; cash is the three-month T-bill.

三：再平衡与成本。所有组合月度再平衡；久期匹配每月重解目标久期，风险平价与盈余最优化每年 1 月用最近 36 个月重解一次权重。成本按权重变动的绝对值计，股票、长债、中债各 2 个基点，短债 1 个，黄金 3 个，商品 5 个，均为单边。

3: rebalancing and costs. Every portfolio rebalances monthly; duration matching re-solves its target monthly, while risk parity and surplus optimisation re-solve each January on the trailing 36 months. Costs are charged on the absolute change in weight: two basis points for equities and both long and intermediate bonds, one for short bonds, three for gold and five for commodities, all one way.

四：与站上其他笔记的关系。数据管线与《1/N 打败最优化》《风险平价实测》《全天候组合实测》《永久组合实测》完全同源，但那几篇是资产端研究，本篇把基准换成了负债；因此组合层面的数字不可直接与那几篇对照。本文的资产端数字（例如纯股年化 10.80%）与《1/N 打败最优化》一致，负债端是本篇新增。

4: relation to the other notes. The data pipeline is the same one behind the 1/N, risk-parity, All Weather and Permanent Portfolio notes, but those are asset-side studies while this one replaces the benchmark with a liability; portfolio-level figures are therefore not directly comparable. The asset-side numbers here (all-equities at 10.80% a year, for instance) match the 1/N note exactly; everything on the liability side is new.

五：已知偏差。（a）单一利率平折现，真实负债用的是分期限贴现曲线；（b）没有退保、续保与年金领取选择权，这类选择权会让负债的凸性比本文更大；（c）恒定到期假设抹掉了新业务带来的久期漂移；（d）资产端没有利率互换与通胀挂钩债券，而这两样是真实负债驱动组合最常用的精细对冲工具；（e）「一年跌破 100%」的概率由 392 个月的经验频率估计，样本只含两个完整加息周期，尾部偏乐观；（f）全部为历史回测，不构成任何真实偿付能力判断。

5: known biases. (a) A single flat discount rate, where real liabilities use a term structure. (b) No lapse, renewal or annuitisation options, which in practice make the liability more convex than modelled here. (c) The constant-maturity assumption removes the duration drift that new business creates. (d) No interest-rate swaps and no inflation-linked bonds, the two instruments real liability-driven portfolios use most for fine hedging. (e) The one-year shortfall probabilities come from 392 months containing only two complete hiking cycles, so the tail is optimistic. (f) These are historical backtests and constitute no statement about any real solvency position.
