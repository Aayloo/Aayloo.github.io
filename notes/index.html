All Weather, tested: does not forecasting actually survive every cycle?全天候组合实测：不预测，真的能穿越周期吗

用 1990-01 至 2025-09 共 428 个月的美国数据检验全天候组合的公开描述。它年化 7.6%、波动 7.6%、最大回撤 −22.3%，比 60/40 稳得多（9.2% / −29.6%），但收益更低（8.9%）夏普更差（0.64 对 0.68）。更关键的是它并没有做到环境中性：40% 的长债贡献了 53.5% 的风险，商品腿只有 1.2%。含 6 张图表与 1 个可拖股票权重的交互动画。 428 monthly US observations test the public description of All Weather: 7.6% a year, −22.3% maximum drawdown, steadier than a 60/40 but with lower return and Sharpe. It is not environment-neutral — long bonds carry 53.5% of the risk while commodities carry 1.2%.

Can investing beat inflation?投资能跑赢通胀吗

用 1990-01 至 2026-08 共 432 个月的美国数据检验「投资能跑赢通胀」。股票年化实际收益 8.4%、债券 1.9%、现金只有 0.1%——最初的 100 元 36 年后只值 102 元，而且持有 20 年仍有六成窗口跑输通胀。2022 年通胀 6.5% 时五类资产实际收益全为负。含 6 张图表与 1 个可拖持有期的交互动画。 432 monthly US observations test whether investing beats inflation. Real annualised returns: equities 8.4%, bonds 1.9%, cash just 0.1% — 100 units of cash became 102 after 36 years, and cash still trailed inflation in 59.9% of twenty-year windows. In 2022 all five assets lost in real terms.

错过最好的十天：一句被误用的统计The ten best days: a statistic used the wrong way

The ten best days: a statistic used the wrong way错过最好的十天：一句被误用的统计

用标普 500 从 1970 年至今 14,309 个交易日检验「错过最好的 10 天，收益腰斩」。全仓年化 8.10%，错过最好 10 天降到 6.52%——但躲过最差 10 天升到 10.12%，幅度还更大。随机剔除 10 天则几乎没影响，而最好的日子与最差的日子只隔几天。含 6 张图表与 1 个可拖天数的交互动画。 14,309 trading days test the claim that missing the ten best days halves your return. Buy-and-hold compounds at 8.10% and missing the best ten cuts it to 6.52% — but avoiding the worst ten lifts it to 10.12%, a bigger move. Missing ten random days changes almost nothing, and the best days sit days away from the worst.

黄金：抗通胀，还是抗波动？Gold: an inflation hedge, or a volatility hedge?

Gold: an inflation hedge, or a volatility hedge?黄金：抗通胀，还是抗波动？

以 1968-01 至 2026-08 共 703 个月的伦敦金价与美国 CPI 检验「黄金抗通胀」。58 年看它确实跑赢（实际购买力 12.5 倍），但 1980 年买在高点要等 45 年才回本，持有十年有四成窗口是亏的。它真正对冲的是组合波动：与股票相关性 −0.08，加入 10% 可把最大回撤从 28.4% 降到 24.6%。含 7 张图表与 1 个可拖权重的交互动画。 703 monthly observations of the London gold price and US CPI test the claim that gold hedges inflation. Over 58 years real purchasing power rose 12.5-fold — but a January 1980 buyer waited 45 years to break even, and 40% of ten-year windows lost money after inflation.

「别人恐惧我贪婪」在收益层面站得住：VIX ≥ 30 之后 12 个月的中位收益是 21.8%。但 36 年半里这样的机会只有 7 个互不重叠的市场阶段，其中一次买入后两年仍在亏损。含 8 张图表与 1 个可拖阈值的交互动画。 "Be greedy when others are fearful" survives on returns: the median 12-month return after VIX ≥ 30 is 21.8%. But 36 years produced only seven independent episodes, one of which was still losing money two years later.
