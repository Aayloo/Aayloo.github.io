60/40 及其变体：最有名的配置规则经得起检验吗The 60/40, tested: does the most famous allocation rule hold up?

The 60/40, tested: does the most famous allocation rule hold up?60/40 及其变体：最有名的配置规则经得起检验吗

2026-10-07 · 17 min

用 1990-01 至 2025-09 共 428 个月的美国数据回测 60/40 及其变体。它年化 9.09%、波动 9.1%、最大回撤 −27.0%、夏普 0.70——而 60% 这个数字恰好落在最差的区间：40–50% 的夏普同为 0.72，最大回撤只有 −21.5%。它真正的失效场景是股债同跌：2022 年两条腿各跌 18.1%，全样本有 13.5% 的月份两条腿同时下跌，CPI 高于 5% 时相关性从 −0.04 翻到 +0.38。把 10% 挪给黄金是最便宜的修补（夏普 0.76、回撤 −22.7%，代价是终值少 4.2%）。含 10 张图表（其中 1 张可拖动股票权重）与 2 张附录表。 428 monthly US observations from 1990-01 to 2025-09 test the 60/40 and its variants: 9.09% a year, 9.1% volatility, a −27.0% maximum drawdown and a 0.70 Sharpe. Sixty percent sits in the worst part of the curve — a 40/60 or 50/50 has the same 0.72 Sharpe with a −21.5% drawdown. What breaks it is stocks and bonds falling together: both legs lost 18.1% in 2022, 13.5% of all months saw both fall, and the correlation flips from −0.04 to +0.38 once CPI is above 5%. Shifting 10% into gold is the cheapest repair (Sharpe 0.76, drawdown −22.7%, at a 4.2% cost to terminal wealth).

The Permanent Portfolio, tested: does an even split cover every environment?永久组合实测：四等分真的能应对任何环境吗

Equal money turns out to be very nearly equal risk (37.5 / 33.9 / 28.4 / 0.2) — but it lost 2.8% a year across the 26 months with CPI above 5%, with gold at −2.95%.

Can investing beat inflation?投资能跑赢通胀吗

Real annualised returns: equities 8.4%, bonds 1.9%, cash just 0.1% — 100 units of cash became 102 after 36 years, and cash still trailed inflation in 59.9% of twenty-year windows. In 2022 all five assets lost in real terms.
