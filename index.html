关于我About

我的工作方式可以概括成一条链：假设 → 数据 → 信号 → 组合 → 风险 → 归因。每个环节都要能说清楚，每个结果都要能被别人重新跑一遍。 My way of working is one chain: hypothesis → data → signal → portfolio → risk → attribution. Every step should be explainable, and every result should be re-runnable by someone else.

这条链拆成两个公开部分：面向投研的 QS 项目集，以及支撑它的量化学习知识库——一边做研究，一边把底层方法补齐。 That chain is split into two public parts: a QS research portfolio, and the quant knowledge base behind it — doing research while keeping the fundamentals sharp.
