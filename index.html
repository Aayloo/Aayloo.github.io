关于我About

数据科学与 AI 工程背景，日常工作围绕数据管道、模型开发与评估，以及研究工具的工程化；工作之外的兴趣集中在系统化投资研究。 Data science and AI engineering, with day-to-day work across data pipelines, model development and evaluation, and research tooling. Outside work, the focus is systematic investment research.

判断一份研究是否值得信，看的是结论在成本、风险与不同样本下是否依然成立，而非回测曲线的形状。公开工作因此分成两块：QS 项目集（信号与组合风险）与方法底座（统计、机器学习、深度学习、LLM）。 What makes a study trustworthy is whether the conclusion still holds under costs, risk and different samples — not the shape of the equity curve. The public work is therefore split into two parts: a QS project portfolio (signals and portfolio risk), and the method stack behind it (statistics, machine learning, deep learning, LLM).

笔记与写作Notes & writing

随研究更新updated as research goes
VALIDATION

为什么单次留出切分会说谎Why a single hold-out split lies

同一份数据换一个切分点，结论就能反转。记录滚动窗口验证在设计上的取舍。 Move the split point and the conclusion flips. Notes on the trade-offs in walk-forward validation design.

COSTS

交易成本假设如何悄悄改写夏普Cost assumptions that quietly rewrite Sharpe

换手、滑点与冲击成本的建模方式，往往比信号本身更影响最终判断。 How turnover, slippage and impact modelling often matter more than the signal itself.

ALT DATA

把文本变成事件因子Turning text into event factors

从抽取到因子化到增量检验的完整链路，以及哪些环节最容易产生虚假信息。 The full chain from extraction to factor construction to incremental testing, and where spurious signal creeps in.

NOTES

不成立的结论同样值得记录Null results deserve a record too

为什么把无效信号写清楚，长期来看比多报一个「有效因子」更有价值。 Why documenting a dead signal is worth more long-term than reporting one more “working factor”.
