01Portfolio baseline

The 60/40, tested: does the most famous allocation rule hold up?

A stronger Sharpe plateau sits below 60% equities, while the real vulnerability appears when stocks and bonds fall together.

↗
02Liability-aware

Liability-driven allocation: change the denominator and the ranking changes

Measuring risk against surplus instead of assets materially changes which portfolio looks appropriate.

↗
03Risk allocation

Risk parity, tested: flatter risk does not automatically mean higher return

The mechanism works, but the realised weights stay close to a naive allocation and the return advantage is conditional.

↗
04Validation

1/N versus the optimiser: when in-sample precision becomes out-of-sample noise

A 1.74 in-sample Sharpe delivered 0.54 out of sample. Most of the gap came from estimation assumptions, not optimisation code.
